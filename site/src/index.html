Common questions

Questions we hear first.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. One fee. Domain and hosting costs go to your providers.

You do. After hand-over we keep no connection to it.

Can I see it before I decide?

Yes. A demo first, then a preview.

What if something breaks later?

Tell us. We quote the fix.

Can you add a form, bookings or a shop?

Yes, as extras quoted per business.

What if I have no logo?

We set your name as a text logo.

Do I have to send photos?

No. Send what you have.

Which languages?

English only, for now.
