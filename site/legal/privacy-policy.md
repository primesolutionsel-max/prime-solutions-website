### 7. How we protect it (POPIA s19)
We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including: HTTPS on this website; multi-factor authentication on our business accounts; a password manager; access only for the founders and team members who need it; encrypted devices; keeping software updated; and written agreements with operators. No system is completely secure.

### 8. If there is a security compromise (POPIA s22)
If we reasonably believe your personal information has been accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, we will notify the Information Regulator and you as soon as reasonably possible, unless a public body responsible for crime prevention says this would impede an investigation. Our notice will tell you what happened, what we are doing and what you can do to protect yourself.

### 9. Your rights (POPIA ss5, 11, 23–25)
You can, free of charge (except a reasonable fee for copies of records where allowed):
- ask whether we hold your information and request a copy (access);
- ask us to correct, update or delete it;
- object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
- withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect processing already done);
- complain to the Information Regulator.

Send requests to the Information Officer at [IO email]. We may need to verify your identity. We aim to reply within [30] days. You may also use the forms prescribed under the POPIA Regulations (Form 1 objection, Form 2 correction/deletion) and the access process in our PAIA Manual (Part G).

### 10. Direct marketing
We send marketing emails only if you **opt in** (unticked checkbox) or, if you're a client, about our own similar services. Every email has an unsubscribe link. Under the POPIA Regulations (as amended 17 April 2025), opting out does not count as consent; we will never treat silence or a pre-ticked box as consent.

This website is for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect information about anyone under 18. If you believe we have, contact us and we'll delete it.
