# Privacy Policy — Prime Solutions EL

**Advertise Only:** nothing is sold on this website, no orders or payments are taken here, and any work is agreed in writing before it starts.

## In short
- This website has **no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking**. It does not ask you for anything.
- We only get your personal information if **you choose to email us**. Our web host may also keep standard server logs.
- We use it only to reply to you, give you a quote and, if you go ahead, build your website. We do not sell it and do not send marketing.
- Only our small team sees it. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail).
- You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or complain to the Information Regulator.

## 1. Who we are and how to contact us
This website is run by Prime Solutions EL ("we", "us"). We decide how the information described here is used, so we are the "responsible party" under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA").
Contact for anything in this policy: {{legal.email}}

## 2. What we receive
- **What you choose to email us:** usually your name, email address and anything you include about your business or project. Please do not send sensitive information (such as health, religion, biometrics or criminal history) or information about children.
- **Server logs:** this website is hosted by {{legal.host}}. The host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, date and time, page requested and browser type, for a limited period. This website sets no cookies.
- **Nothing else:** the site has no forms, sign-up, login, cookies, local storage, analytics, payments, or content loaded from other companies (fonts are stored on our own site). The "copy" buttons only copy our details to your own clipboard.

## 3. Why we use it
This website only advertises our website-building service. It does not collect information through forms, accounts, payments or tracking. If you email us, we use your details only to answer you, to prepare a quote if you ask, and, if you go ahead, to build your website. We do this because you contacted us and asked, and because we have a genuine interest in answering the people who write to us. We do not use your email address for marketing or send you advertising. Server logs are used only to keep the site running and secure. We will not use your information for anything else without telling you.

## 4. Who sees it
- Our small team, who answer your email and quote or build for you.
- Google (Gmail), which provides our email and stores messages for us. Google may store and process email on servers in other countries, including outside South Africa. Google is bound by its own data-protection terms.
- Our web host, {{legal.host}}, for server logs only.
We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## 5. How long we keep it
We keep enquiry emails in our email account so we can find an earlier conversation if you write again, and for no other purpose. Enquiries that do not lead to any work are deleted 12 months after our last contact. Server logs are kept by the host for a limited period.

## 6. Security
We keep your emails in a password-protected account that only our team can access, with two-step login switched on, and the site uses HTTPS. No system is completely secure; if we believe your information was accessed without permission, we will tell you and the Information Regulator.

## 7. Your rights
You may ask us to tell you what we hold, give you a copy, correct it or delete it, or object to us using it. Email {{legal.email}}. We may need to check it is really you, and we will reply within 30 days.

## 8. Complaints
Please contact us first. You may also complain to the **Information Regulator (South Africa)**:

## 9. Children
This website is meant for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect information about anyone under 18; if you think we have, tell us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes
If this website changes what it collects, we will update this policy first and change the "last updated" date.
