# How It Works, Refunds and Cancellation — Prime Solutions EL


**Advertise Only:** nothing is sold on this website. You cannot order or pay for anything here. All work is agreed in writing before it starts.

## How it works
1. **Email us.** Tell us about your business and the kind of website you would like. Our address is {{legal.email}}.
2. **We send you a written quote and agreement.** It sets out what we will build, the price, timing and the terms that apply.
3. **You accept in writing, and any deposit is set out in your written quote and agreement.** Nothing is agreed until you accept the written quote and agreement. You are free to ask questions or say no.
4. **We build and hand over.** We make your website and hand it over as set out in the agreement.

## Prices
The prices on this website are guide prices, and a written quote comes first. Your actual price is set out in your written quote. No VAT is charged, because Prime Solutions EL is not registered for VAT. Hosting, your domain name and any other ongoing costs are your own, and we charge no monthly fee.

## Deposits, refunds and cancellation
Because nothing is sold on this website, no deposit, refund or cancellation applies to anything on this website itself.

If you decide to go ahead, any deposit, the fee arrangements, refunds, cancellation and any cooling-off period are set out in your written agreement **before any work starts**. Please read them before you accept.

## Your rights under South African law
Nothing on this page or in any agreement removes rights you have under South African law. This includes your rights under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (for example, the right to fair and reasonable terms) and, where they apply, the cooling-off and other rights in the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002.
