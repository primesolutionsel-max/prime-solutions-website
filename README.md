# Prime Solutions website

**This repository is the master** for the Prime Solutions website: Premium, Pro, Standard and the legal pages. It is **Advertise Only**: the site shows what Prime Solutions offers and how to email us; nothing is bought on it, and it has no forms, payments, sign-ups, analytics, cookies or tracking.

| Folder | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `site/` | The website project: `site.json` (all the wording and the settings), `legal/` (one source file per legal page), `build.py`, `src/`, `CREDITS.md`, `OPEN-MARKERS.md` and the finished `dist/` folder that gets uploaded. Premium is at the main address, Standard at `/standard/`, Pro at `/pro/`. **`site/README.md` explains how to build and change it.** |
| `assets/` | `hero-test.jpg`, a test hero banner (dark green, space on the left for a headline) |
| `review/` | `mobile-review.md` and screenshots from an earlier phone and desktop review |
| `downloads/` | An old zip of an earlier build. Out of date; do not use it. |

The legal pages are written in `site/legal/` and are placeholders until the real text is supplied. Anything still to be supplied is listed in `site/OPEN-MARKERS.md`.

This repository is public for now. Do not add passwords, keys, client details or prices that should stay private.
